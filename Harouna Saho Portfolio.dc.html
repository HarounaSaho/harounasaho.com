SEC · 03 / INFO

A painter building one world, scene by scene.

Harouna Saho is a senior at Yale, painting out of New Haven. He is from The Gambia by way of Norman, Oklahoma, and the distance between those places is most of what the work is about.

The paintings are concept art for one continuing world. There is no plot and no name for it - each canvas is a scene, and the world gets larger the way a location does, not the way a story does.
